# ADR-129 — v10 power-management restoration, per-cell bypass diodes, and the hub panel connector

- **Status:** **ACCEPTED — operator instruction, 2026-10-22** (verbatim directives quoted below)
- **Decision owner:** Felix (operator)
- **Supersedes (in part):** the v10 component set as generated by `make_v10_netlist.py`
- **Related:** ADR-006 (harvest storage + supercap balancing), ADR-029 D8 (F33 rail selector),
  ADR-047 (F33 PA rail provision), ADR-051 §2.1/§2.7 (hub array + hub-side bypass diodes),
  ADR-054 §2.2 (why bypass diodes must be hub-side), ADR-118 (v10 directives), ADR-128
  (schematic is the source of truth), ADR-044 (over-provisioning).
- **Reference:** `tracker/hardware/connectivity/v10_pinmap.reg` (the source of truth),
  `tracker/hardware/schematics/flight_board/build_flight_sch.py` (`V9_COMPONENTS` / `V9_NETS`).

## The problem this ADR resolves

v10 was generated by transforming v9's netlist as text. That transform carried **one end of
seventeen nets and dropped the other end**, because **43 components present on v9 are absent
from v10**. KiCad ERC reported the symptom as 17 `label_dangling` errors; the cause was missing
parts, not a drawing defect.

```
v9 components: 59        v10 components: 20        dropped: 43
```

This ADR decides each dropped group, one group at a time, as the operator instructed:
*"Let's decide on a case by case basis."*

## The operator's directives, verbatim in substance (2026-10-22)

1. **Power management: include.** *"I want the power management ones for sure. Include the
   voltage regulator and the diodes for bypassing dead pannels."*
2. **Per-cell bypass diodes: include.** *"All solar cells should have bypass diodes."*
3. **Hub solar cell: connector only, no on-hub land.** *"Let's include a connector for the hub
   solar cell, but don't make space for it on the hub. Its an external panel like the wing
   boards."*
4. **The bare SX1280 matching network: leave out.** *"Please leave out that matching network.
   Its part of the SX1280 board now."*
5. **Two layers if possible.** *"It would be great if we can make due with two layers and we
   don't need 4."*

## Decisions

### D1 — POWER MANAGEMENT: RESTORED (18 refs)

| ref | value | footprint | function | authority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `J_VCC` | 3-pad solder jumper | SolderJumper-3_P1.3mm_Open | F33 PA rail select: VSCAP / +3V3 | **ADR-029 D8, operator-ratified** |
| `D1` | BAT54 | SOD-123 | series blocking, solar→supercap (blocks night back-feed) | ADR-006 |
| `R_BAL1`, `R_BAL2` | 10 k | R_0402 | supercap balancing (2 cells in series) | **ADR-006 Accepted** |
| `C10` | 10 µF | C_0402 | +3V3 bulk (LDO stability, ESP32 TX) | ADR-006 |
| `C_BULK` | 100 µF | C_1206 | bulk at the F33 VCC pin (1.2 A step within 20 mV) | ADR-047 §4 |
| `C_HF` | 100 nF | C_0402 | HF bypass at the F33 pin | ADR-047 §4 |
| `R_F1` | 100 R | R_0402 | GNSS rail RC filter (series) | ADR-029 §2(d) |
| `C4` | 10 µF | C_0402 | GNSS rail bulk on the filtered feed | ADR-029 §2(d) |
| `R_DIV1`, `R_DIV2` | 1 M | R_0402 | supercap-rail ADC divider | ADR-006 |
| `R_MON1`, `R_MON2` | 4.7 M | R_0402 | F33 rail monitor divider (ratio 0.5) | ADR-047 §6 |
| `C_MON` | 10 nF | C_0402 | ADC sample-and-hold reservoir (tau 23.5 ms) | ADR-047 §6 |
| `U7` | TPS7A02 | SOT-23-5 | 3.3 V LDO | **already on v10** |

**`J_VCC` is load-bearing and is the reason `F33_VCC` was dangling.** It is a three-pad solder
jumper: pin 1 = `VSCAP` (raw supercap, pre-LDO), pin 2 = COM → `F33_VCC`, pin 3 = `+3V3`.
The F33's 5 V PA rail has **three possible sources and no default**; without the jumper the rail
has no source at all. ADR-029 D8 made this jumper mandatory and operator-ratified it.

### D2 — BYPASS DIODES: ALL SOLAR CELLS GET ONE

Per the operator directive *"All solar cells should have bypass diodes."* This covers **both**
the four wing interfaces and the hub array.

**Wing interfaces — `D_BP1..D_BP4`, SS24 (2 A / 40 V), SMA:**

```
D_BP1  K→W1_SOLAR_P  A→W1_SOLAR_N
D_BP2  K→W1_SOLAR_N  A→W2_SOLAR_N
D_BP3  K→W2_SOLAR_N  A→W3_SOLAR_N
D_BP4  K→W3_SOLAR_N  A→GND
```

- **HUB-SIDE, not wing-side (ADR-054 §2.2, adversarial finding F3).** A cut jettisons the wing
  *and every part soldered to it*, so a wing-board diode leaves with the wing and cannot bridge
  the gap it was fitted to bridge. Without a hub-side diode a cutter flight is **worse than
  baseline**: the first cut opens the string and the array delivers **0.00 W**.
- **SS24 class, not BAT54 (ADR-051 §2.4/§2.7).** Post-cut the surviving wings drive the array's
  **full 1.2 A continuously for the rest of the flight** — a permanent bypass duty, not the
  shading transient a 200 mA part is sold for. Reverse rating must clear the ~9.58 V cold
  open-circuit stack.
- **SMA land, not SOD-323 (ADR-051 §2.7).** SOD-323 cannot carry 2 A.

**Hub array — `D_HUB1`, `D_HUB2`, SS24, SMA:** one bypass Schottky per hub-array cell, cathode
to the cell's + node, anode to its − node, at the same SS24 class so a hub-cell failure is
contained by a path that survives any wing cut.

### D3 — HUB SOLAR CELL: CONNECTOR ON-HUB, CELL OFF-HUB

The hub array is an **external panel, like the wing boards**. v10 carries:

- a **connector** for the hub panel (its mechanical form is an OPEN item, OPEN-26), and
- the **bypass diodes** `D_HUB1`/`D_HUB2` on the hub side, per D2, and
- the existing `U_HUB_CVT` converter input pads (`HUB_PV_P` / `GND` / `HUB_PV_N`).

v10 does **not** carry the cells `PVA1`/`PVA2` themselves, and **no board area is reserved for
them**. The cell count, mounting plane, harvester part and outline remain OPEN (OPEN-26).

`D_HUB1`/`D_HUB2` remain hub-side for the same reason as D2: a diode on the external panel
leaves with the panel.

**Consequence:** `HUB_PV_MID1` (the interior node between `D_HUB1`/`D_HUB2`) becomes a real net
on the board, between the connector and the diodes. The connector's pin count is an OPEN item
and must be decided before the connector is placed.

### D4 — BARE SX1280 MATCHING NETWORK: NOT RESTORED

`U4` (bare SX1280) and its support network — `C0`, `C1`, `C2`, `C3`, `C5`, `C6`, `C7`, `C8`,
`C9`, `L1`, `L2`, `L4`, `Q1`, `R15` — are **obsolete and must not be restored**.

ADR-118 directive 3 replaced the bare QFN SX1280 + scattered 7-part network with the
**E28-2G4M27SX module**, whose matching network, PA, LNA and 52 MHz crystal are inside the
module: *"the whole E28 board with the matching network included so that we don't need to design
that ourselves."* Restoring these parts would undo that decision and re-add a network measured
fatal in `docs/fab/SX1280-MODULE-DECISION.md`.

**Note on the two apparent dangling nets in this group:** `ANT2_2G4_RANGE` and `ANT_C_2G4` are
**drawing artefacts of the v9→v10 text transform**, not real gaps. Both connect to module pads
that DO exist on v10 (`ANT2.1` and `U_C.10`); the net simply has one visible endpoint because
the v9 side of the pair was on `U4`/`U3`. They close when the schematic draws the module pads
they attach to.

### D5 — WING CUTTERS: `U_CUT1..U_CUT4` — DEFERRED, PENDING OPERATOR DECISION

Not decided by this ADR. The four cut channels are absent from v10 and are the sole reason
`CUT_SENSE_W1..W4` are dangling. Each carries a nichrome drive **and** cut-sense on its wing's
one reserved `RF_FEED` conductor, fed from **`VSCAP` pre-LDO** so a cut works with the converter
dead (ADR-047 §2.1). The drive is **mandated to be a latched one-shot** (ADR-051 §2.5.1):
cutting is irreversible and a level-sensitive driver can re-fire on reset, brown-out, retry or a
floating gate, each fire costing ~2.59 J of the bank's 16.63 J. Firmware must serialise cuts
(4 simultaneous fires = 9.9 A = 2.4× the IRLML2502 class rating). Part is `TODO(unverified)`
(OPEN-25, OPEN-28).

### D6 — `D_CLAMP`: LAND IT, LEAVE IT DNP

Over-voltage TVS on `F33_VCC`. ADR-044 option (c) + ADR-047 §7 made it *required* by the
operator's over-provisioning directive. **The set point is not publishable**: it must sit above
the 5.4 V rail top and below the F33's damage threshold, and **Rev 1.1 of the datasheet does not
publish the damage threshold** → part AND set point are `TODO(unverified)`.

**Decision: provide the land, mark it DNP, do not guess a part.** A wrong clamp is worse than no
clamp.

### D7 — NTC PROVISION: NOT RESTORED

`R_NTC1`, `TH_NTC1` were **DNP on v9** and the owned bare LR2021 has **no NTC castellation**
(OPEN-29), so the compensation loop cannot close. No land on v10 until a module revision breaks
the pin out (ADR-058 D6).

## Consequences

- **v10 component count: 20 → ~38** (D1's 18 refs, of which `U7` already exists, plus D2's
  `D_HUB1/2` and `D_BP1..4`, plus the hub connector).
- **Four SMA footprints** (`D_BP1..D_BP4`) plus two more (`D_HUB1/2`) are the largest area items
  in this set (~4.3 × 2.6 mm each). Density on a 72 × 80 mm two-sided board rises materially.
- **The 17 dangling nets are mostly closed by this ADR:** `F33_VCC` (via `J_VCC`), `GNSS_VCC`
  (via `R_F1`/`C4`), `HUB_PV_P`/`HUB_PV_N` (via the hub connector + `D_HUB1/2`),
  `W1_SOLAR_P` (via `D1`/`D_BP1`), `F33_VSENSE` (via `R_MON1/2`/`C_MON`). `CUT_SENSE_W1..W4`
  and `IO0` remain open pending D5 and OPEN-16.
- **This is a PLACEMENT change and therefore requires a fresh ADR-120 R3 human placement gate**,
  with renders, before any routing resumes.
- **Two-layer target (operator directive 5) is not yet decided.** The E28 cross-side escape bug
  is fixed (B46); the capacity question must be re-measured with a clean router before a
  four-layer order is considered. A four-layer stackup roughly doubles small-run fab cost and is
  the operator's call.
