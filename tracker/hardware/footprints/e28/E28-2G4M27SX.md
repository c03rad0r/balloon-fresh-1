## Keep-outs (datasheet §4.1 — binding per ADR-119/R1)

- No traces under the module except the digital corner, and those on the far layer with top copper shield-ground under the contact area.
- Keep away from power/high-frequency traces on **both sides** at the site.
- Antenna: on-module IPEX — the board carries **no RF network** for this radio. Route a short U.FL-to-IPEX jumper or mount the antenna feed at the board edge near pin side.
